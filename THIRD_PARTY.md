# Third-party notices

## Mana (icon font) — card type icons
Card-type icons (planeswalker, creature, sorcery, instant, artifact, enchantment, land, token) in `src/typeIconSvgs.ts` come from the **Mana** icon font by Andrew Gioia (https://github.com/andrewgioia/mana), licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1** (https://scripts.sil.org/OFL). The glyphs are embedded as SVG; the colour is set to `currentColor`.

## Scryfall — mana symbols
`src/symbolSvgs.ts` embeds Scryfall's mana symbol SVGs (https://scryfall.com/docs/api/card-symbols), generated by `scripts/gen-symbols.mjs`. Magic: The Gathering and its symbols are property of Wizards of the Coast; this plugin is unofficial fan content.

## Philosopher (font) — card name and power/toughness on the battle board
`styles.css` embeds the Latin subset of **Philosopher Bold** (by Jovanny Lemonad, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Philosopher) as a base64 `@font-face`. It is used for the card name and power/toughness on the battle board's "original frame" card style, as a free stand-in for the look of Magic's card font. Licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1** (https://scripts.sil.org/OFL). Japanese text falls back to the system Mincho font (Hiragino Mincho ProN / Yu Mincho).
